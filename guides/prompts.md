# Prompts that worked

Type these in Claude Code (after installing the skill). Plain language is fine; Claude turns it into settings and code.

**Pictures and video**

- `/depth-ghost` + a photo
- `/depth-ghost show the folds, bring the background back`
- `/depth-ghost ~/Desktop/clip.mov` (renders a preview first, then the full clip)
- "The mic in the foreground is way too white; give a bit more detail everywhere."
- "Smooth the video out: when the lighting changes, it should be much cleaner."
- "Treat every new shot as a new clip; don't smooth across cuts."

**Music video**

- "Make it dynamic: handheld camera, tracking, zooms, whips, pans, tilts. Here's the audio; line it up at the start, cut it when the video ends, and keep only my track."
- "On the three-count 808 + open hat, key crash zooms and pulsating zooms. When the drop comes, zoom in on the hit."
- "Add lens flares and light streaks, a long-exposure trail that follows the singer."
- "Make it more strobe-y. Switch to the real footage for impact and back to the ghost."
- "Add posterize time; it's too fluid."
- "Add slow-mo and speed-ups at cool moments, more extreme."
- "Do a separate re-edit: cut and rearrange so nothing is stagnant, with layering and masking."
- "Make it in 9:16 and 1:1 too."

**TikTok**

- "Use the remix as the reference: make 10 TikTok clips on different moments of the song. The hook starts with '…' and is 8 bars. Make sure the masks are well integrated."
- "Have two of them loop perfectly when the video repeats."
- "Generate 4 stills of the characters in depth ghost, plus a last carousel slide that sends people to my GitHub, with a Claude Code symbol."
